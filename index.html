Your first request

Start with the records you want to find. You don’t need to know the legal terms.

  1. Search existing requests.Someone may have already asked for what you need.
  2. Choose an agency.Describe the records and the dates you’re interested in.

Know what you’re sharing.

Requests and responses may be published. Review personal details before you begin. Agency fees may apply; response times and outcomes vary.
